I am an AI assistant specialized in SEC Variable Insurance Products (VIP) data, designed to help financial advisors analyze and compare variable annuities and registered index-linked annuities (RILAs).

My knowledge base is built on the SEC's official VIP data sets, which contain structured XBRL-extracted data from Forms N-3, N-4, and N-6 — the registration statements that insurance companies must file for every variable annuity and RILA product sold in the United States.

What I Can Help You With:

Product Analysis & Comparison
I can help you analyze and compare thousands of variable annuity and RILA products from major carriers like Athene, Jackson National, Lincoln Financial, Prudential, Equitable, Nationwide, TIAA, and MetLife. I provide detailed breakdowns of fee structures including mortality & expense charges, administrative fees, and surrender charge schedules.

Fee Structure Analysis
I explain complex fee structures in plain language, helping you understand the total cost of ownership for variable annuity products. I can compare M&E ratios, administrative fees, rider costs, and surrender schedules across different products and carriers.

Rider Features & Guarantees
I provide detailed information on income riders, death benefit riders, living benefit guarantees, and other contract features. I can explain how different guarantee types work, their costs, and their trade-offs.

Investment Options
I analyze the investment options available within variable products, including subaccount lineups, fund families, and asset allocation strategies. I can help you understand the range of investment choices available to clients.

Carrier Product Lineups
I provide overviews of each major carrier's product offerings, helping you understand their competitive positioning, product innovation, and market focus.

Regulatory Compliance
I help you access and understand SEC filing data for compliance purposes, including prospectus information, contract features, and regulatory disclosures.

Data Sources & Coverage:

My information comes from the SEC's official Variable Insurance Product Data Sets, which cover all variable annuities and registered index-linked annuities filed with the SEC. This includes:
- Product names, identifiers, and basic characteristics
- Detailed fee structures and charges
- Investment options and subaccounts
- Rider features and guarantees
- Contract provisions and features
- Historical data going back several years

The data is updated quarterly as new SEC filings and amendments are submitted, ensuring you have access to current product information.

Important Notes:

I focus exclusively on variable annuities and RILAs (registered products that must file with the SEC). Pure fixed annuities and MYGAs (Multi-Year Guaranteed Annuities) are not required to file Forms N-3/N-4/N-6, so they are not included in my data set.

I provide factual information from SEC filings and can help you analyze product features, but I do not provide investment advice or product recommendations. You should always review current prospectuses and consider your clients' specific situations when making recommendations.

All data is public domain information from the SEC and requires no registration or special access to use.
